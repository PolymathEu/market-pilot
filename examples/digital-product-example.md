# Example: Digital Product, Limited Budget

Illustrative only. Not a literal template.

**Input context (abbreviated):** Online course teaching freelance copywriters how to raise their rates, $197 one-time, creator has 8,000 email subscribers and 4,000 Instagram followers, launched twice via email, no paid ad budget, wants to grow revenue without ad spend.

---

## Business Diagnosis

The constraint here isn't audience size, it's launch frequency and conversion mechanism. Two launches in the product's life, against an 8,000-person list, is a small sample to judge the offer by, and a pure "wait for a launch window" model leaves most of the list's buying intent untapped between launches.

## Marketing Opportunity Radar

Biggest Growth Opportunity: moving from launch-only selling to an always-on evergreen funnel off the existing email list, since the audience already exists and isn't being monetized continuously.

Biggest Marketing Weakness: no visible retention or upsell path after the $197 purchase, so each customer's value is capped at one transaction.

Quickest Win: a short, automated 3-5 email sequence for new subscribers who haven't bought yet, addressing the most common objection (likely "will this actually work for my niche," based on the course topic) and linking to a lower-friction entry point.

Biggest Competitive Advantage: creator's own personal brand and track record as a freelance copywriter, which a faceless competitor course can't replicate; this is underused if marketing leans generic.

Highest Priority Action: build the evergreen email sequence first, since it monetizes the existing audience without new content, ad spend, or audience growth required.

## Recommended Strategy

1. Build an always-on 5-email automated sequence for new subscribers, moving from problem awareness to the course offer, running continuously rather than only during launch windows.
2. Add one simple upsell or community offer post-purchase to extend customer value past the single $197 transaction.
3. Lean harder into the creator's personal story and specific results in all messaging, since that's the real differentiator against generic "learn copywriting" content.

## Action Plan

First: write and automate the 5-email sequence this month using existing launch copy as raw material rather than starting from scratch.
Second: test one simple post-purchase upsell (a community, template pack, or coaching add-on) with the next 20 buyers before building anything elaborate.
Third: revisit launch cadence once the evergreen sequence has a few weeks of data to compare against.

## Success Metrics

Email-to-purchase conversion rate on the automated sequence, revenue per subscriber, upsell take rate.

## Final Verdict

Stop treating this as a launch-only business. The list already exists; the fastest growth lever is monetizing it continuously, not finding a bigger list.

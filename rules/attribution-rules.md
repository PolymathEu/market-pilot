# Attribution Rules

Applies whenever ad platform data, channel ROAS, CPA or conversion counts are discussed.

1. Platform-reported results are attributed results. Label them "platform reported" every time, with the platform and attribution window when known.
2. Never add conversions or revenue across Meta, TikTok, Google or any other platforms. Each platform claims credit using its own rules and windows; the same sale is often counted by more than one. Only deduplicated business data (store orders, CRM, payment records) can be summed.
3. Identify overlapping or different attribution windows (for example 7 day click versus 1 day click). Results with different windows are not comparable.
4. Attributed is not incremental. Attributed revenue includes sales that would have happened anyway. Do not call attributed results "caused by" the channel without incrementality evidence (holdout, geo test, lift study, or a before and after with a control).
5. Before any budget recommendation, state the attribution limitation in one line and say what evidence would resolve it. Platform ROAS alone is never sufficient to recommend scaling a channel.
6. Compare channel mix against deduplicated business totals: blended ROAS (total business revenue ÷ total marketing spend) and total new customers. If platform conversions summed exceed business orders, say the overlap is proven.
7. View-through and click-through conversions differ. If the export does not say which is included, record it as unknown.
8. Organic, direct, email and repeat orders are not paid conversions. Do not use total orders as the denominator of a paid CPA.
9. Recommend measurement fixes (consistent UTM tags, one attribution window across platforms, order level source capture, holdout tests) when attribution is the binding constraint.
